## Keyword Density Guidelines

For 1500-word article:

**Primary Keyword:** 8-12 times (0.5-0.8%)
- In H1, H2, first paragraph, conclusion
- Natural placement throughout

**Secondary Keywords:** 4-6 times each (0.25-0.4%)
- In H3s, subheadings, body text

**Related Terms:** 10-15 total mentions
- Variations, synonyms, related concepts

**LSI Keywords (Latent Semantic Indexing):**
For "Pandiya Dynasty" include:
- Tamil kings, Madurai, Meenkodi, Sangam period
- Ancient Tamil, South India, Moovendhar
- Temples, architecture, inscriptions
